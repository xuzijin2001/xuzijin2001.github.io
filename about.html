About Us

Who We Are

Bilingual Early Educators Alliance brings together educators, institutions, community partners, and advocates who believe bilingual educators play an essential role in creating inclusive learning environments for multilingual children and families.

Our Story

Building This Community, Together

Bilingual Early Educators Alliance was created to bring bilingual early childhood educators together — to learn from one another, grow professionally, and strengthen the multilingual communities they serve.

A note on our story: as BEEA grows, we'll add more detail here about our founding and the people behind this work. We're keeping this section intentionally simple for now rather than adding specifics before they're ready to share.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower bilingual early educators with the knowledge, professional connections, resources, and opportunities they need to grow and lead.

Our Vision

We envision a future where bilingual educators are recognized, supported, and equipped to thrive — and where every child's language and cultural identity is valued as a strength.

Our Values

What Guides Our Work

Equity

Expanding fair access to opportunity for every bilingual educator.

Community

Building genuine connection among educators, families, and partners.

Multilingualism

Valuing every language as an asset, not a barrier.

Professional Growth

Investing in the ongoing development of every educator.

Collaboration

Working alongside institutions and organizations that share our goals.

Cultural Responsiveness

Honoring the cultures and identities of children, families, and educators.

Why It Matters

Why Bilingual Education Matters

Bilingual educators help bridge language and culture between schools, children, and families — supporting children's multilingual development, creating culturally responsive classrooms, and strengthening trust between educators and the families they serve.
