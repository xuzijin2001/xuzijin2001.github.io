Who we areQuiénes somos

Building a Stronger Bilingual Early Education Community

Bilingual Early Educators Alliance Corp. is a Boston-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to strengthening and supporting the bilingual early childhood education workforce — through professional development, educational pathways, community, and partnership.

Bilingual Educators Do More Than Teach in Two Languages

They build bridges between children, families, and schools — and help every child see multilingualism as a strength.

  • Build bridges between children, families, and schools
  • Support children's language development
  • Help preserve cultural identity
  • Strengthen communication with multilingual families
  • Create inclusive learning environments
  • Help children see multilingualism as a strength
